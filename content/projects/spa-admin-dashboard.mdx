An admin dashboard built for a spa business to manage invoices, customers, and revenue at a glance. It's based on the Next.js "App Router Course" dashboard pattern, extended with real authentication — NextAuth v5, Vercel Postgres, and bcrypt-hashed credentials — and rebranded end to end for the business it runs.

Built with Next.js 14, NextAuth v5 beta, Vercel Postgres, and Tailwind CSS.
