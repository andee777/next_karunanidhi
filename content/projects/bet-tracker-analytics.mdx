Bet Tracker & Analytics is a dashboard for tracking sports bets and the performance of automated betting bots ("robots") across football and basketball, including live odds. Each robot gets its own performance page with profit-trend charts and balance tracking, and odds update in real time over WebSocket routes.

Built with Next.js 15, both Prisma and Drizzle for different parts of the data layer, Postgres, JWT-based auth, and Chart.js/Recharts/lightweight-charts alongside TanStack Table and Virtual for the dense data views. See also [Bet Data Validator](/projects/bet-data-validator), the companion tool that keeps its underlying sports data clean.
