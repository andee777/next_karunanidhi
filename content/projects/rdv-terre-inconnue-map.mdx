An interactive Leaflet map built as a companion to *Rendez-vous en Terre Inconnue*, the French travel documentary series that sends a guest to live with a remote indigenous community. Markers cluster as you zoom out and split apart as you zoom in, making it easy to browse the show's locations across the globe.

Built with Next.js 15, `react-leaflet` with the marker-cluster plugin, and `supercluster` for clustering performance.
